Electrical & Electronic Engineering • UMaT

A+ Hours was built as a solution against the highly distracting nature of modern digital environments. It provides a clean, simple space for deep work, using the Pomodoro technique wrapped in a premium interface.

As an engineering student at the University of Mines and Technology (UMaT), I saw the need for a tool that bridges tough academic study with a smooth, fast digital experience. This app is the result of that idea.

Built completely independent of heavy frameworks, A+ Hours uses a smart offline-first setup, making sure your study flow is never broken by bad internet connections.

Data & Privacy Architecture

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Local Processing & Ownership

By default, A+ Hours operates strictly offline. All your data—including study times, points (XP), courses, and chat history—is stored safely right here inside your browser. You own all of your data.

2. Cloud Sync (Optional)

To save your data across different devices, you can choose to create an account and log in. When you do, your data is securely sent to our cloud just to keep it backed up. We never sell or share your study data with anyone else.

3. No Tracking

This app uses zero third-party trackers, ads, or cookies. It is built to be private, making sure your study habits are completely your business.

Support the Project

A+ Hours is completely ad-free and built independently. If this tool has helped you focus and get better grades, consider supporting server costs and future updates.
